Privacy Policy

Your privacy is as important as your skincare journey. We are committed to protecting your personal information and ensuring complete transparency in how your data is collected and used.

Information we collect

We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, telephone number, billing address and payment details when you place an order, create an account or subscribe to The Glow Club newsletter.

How we use your information

Your information allows us to process your orders, provide customer support, improve our services and personalise your shopping experience. With your permission, we may also send exclusive product launches, offers and beauty updates.

Protecting your data

We are committed to protecting your personal information. Appropriate security measures are used to safeguard your data against unauthorised access, misuse or disclosure.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies to improve functionality, remember your preferences and provide a smoother browsing experience. You may manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Third-party services

Certain trusted third-party providers may assist with payment processing, delivery services and website analytics. These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their services and are expected to protect your data.

Your rights

You have the right to request access to your personal data, correct inaccurate information or request the removal of your information where appropriate, subject to applicable laws.

Contact us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the way your information is handled, please contact our customer support team through the Contact page. We are always happy to assist you.
